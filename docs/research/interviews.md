# Week-0 demand interviews

Goal: decide whether to build past Phase 1. **Proceed if at least 7 of 10 learners say they would try
it before committing, or at least 1 instructor would require it in a course.** Otherwise rescope.

## Who

- 10 juniors or students who use an AI coding assistant at least weekly.
- 2–3 instructors, bootcamp mentors or open-source maintainers.

## What to show

A 30-second terminal recording (or `kyd check --dry-run` on their own repo) plus a mock-up of one
question, one wrong answer, and the hint that follows.

## Script (15 minutes)

1. When did you last commit code an AI wrote that you could not fully explain? What happened?
2. (Show the demo.) What is your first reaction?
3. Would you run this before committing? Every commit, some commits, or never? Why?
4. How long could a check take before you would turn it off?
5. Would you want it to block your commit, or only nudge?
6. Would you attach an "understanding receipt" to a PR? Who would you want to see it?
7. What would make you uninstall it?
8. Instructors/maintainers only: would you require learning mode in a course or for first-time
   contributors? What would you need to trust the grading?
